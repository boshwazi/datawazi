Payment schedules

Audit / workshop100% upfront (audit may be 50 / 50)
Smaller build50% signing · 50% go-live
Larger build40% signing · 40% design sign-off or build complete · 20% go-live
GLC / govt, no depositPO first, then e.g. 30 / 30 / 30 / 10 milestones on their payment terms
RetainerMonthly in advance, minimum 3 months

Getting paid

  • Invoices due in 14 days (GLC: their terms, PO required)
  • Invoice no. DW-INV-YYYY-NNN; quote no. on the SOW; SOW and PO no. on every invoice
  • Log every invoice in Payment_Tracker.xlsx; review overdue every Monday
  • Chase: day 1 → email 12 · day 7 → email 13 · day 14 → call sponsor, pause if allowed

Never

  • Start work before the first payment clears
  • Quote prices on the fit-check call
  • Publish prices or payment terms (website, social, info pack)
  • Invoice hypercare separately
  • Delete or reuse an invoice number
