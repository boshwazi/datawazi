# Security & data protection at datawazi

**datawazi** · data & AI solutions · Malaysia · hello@datawazi.com

> 🛈 Client-facing summary of `internal/policies/information-security-and-data-protection-policy.md`. Send with proposals, or when a client's IT, procurement or legal team asks how we handle data. Keep it in line with the internal policy.

We build systems that touch our clients' most important data. This page explains how we protect it.

## How we handle your data
- **Your environment, your ownership.** We build in **your own cloud accounts and systems** wherever possible. Code, data, dashboards and documentation belong to you.
- **Least access.** We ask for the minimum access the project needs, read-only where possible, with a **named account for each person**. No shared logins.
- **Agreements first.** We sign an **NDA** before discovery, and a **Data Processing Agreement** before touching personal data. A **PDPA checklist** is completed together at kickoff.
- **Personal data minimised.** We prefer aggregated or anonymised data, and mask personal fields in development and test environments.
- **Data stays where you need it.** Storage location is agreed upfront. No transfer outside Malaysia without your written approval.
- **AI used responsibly.** Your personal or confidential data is never entered into AI tools without your written approval, and only in business tools that don't train on your data.
- **Clean exit.** At the end of the engagement we delete any of your data we hold, ask you to remove our access, and confirm both in writing.

## How we protect our own systems
- Password manager and **multi-factor authentication** on all business accounts
- **Encrypted devices** with automatic updates and screen locks
- Business email and approved tools only; no client data in chat apps or personal storage
- Private code repositories; no passwords or keys in code
- Everyone working on your project signs a confidentiality agreement

## If something goes wrong
We have a written incident response plan. If an incident may affect your data, **we notify you within 24 hours** and give you the information you need to meet your own obligations under the PDPA, including notifying the Commissioner where required.

## Standards we follow
- Malaysia's **Personal Data Protection Act 2010** and its **2024 amendments**
- Your own IT and security policies, which we follow on your systems
- A written information security and data protection policy, reviewed every year
