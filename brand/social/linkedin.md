## About
datawazi is a data and AI solutions consultancy based in Malaysia.

We design, build and run the data and AI systems that help organisations work smarter: data platforms, data pipelines and integrations, dashboards and analytics, workflow automation, custom internal tools, and practical AI such as knowledge assistants and AI features.

We work with any organisation that has data and decisions to make: SMEs, corporates, public sector and GLCs, non-profits, education, healthcare and startups.

How we work:
→ Business first: decisions before technology.
→ Practical, not hype: working systems, honest scoping.
→ Built to hand over: documentation and training in every project.
→ Vendor-neutral: we recommend what fits, not what we resell.
→ PDPA-aware: personal data protection designed in from day one.

The name: wazi is Swahili for "clear", and to Malaysian ears it echoes wazir, a trusted adviser. Data made clear, from a trusted adviser.
