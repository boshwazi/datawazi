[07] // questions

Straight answers.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, so we don't guess. After a free fit check we either quote a clearly scoped piece of work, or recommend a short paid readiness audit that ends in a roadmap with costs for each step.

We don't know where to start. Can you help?

Yes. That's what the readiness audit is for. In 1–2 weeks we review your systems and goals and give you a prioritised roadmap you can act on, with us or without us.

Do you only work with one platform?

No. We're vendor-neutral and work with Microsoft, Google, AWS, open-source and the tools you already have. If you're "a Microsoft shop", we build on Microsoft.

How do you handle personal data?

PDPA is part of kickoff: we agree what personal data is used, who can access it, how it's masked and where it's stored, including keeping it in Malaysia where required.

What happens after go-live?

You get documentation, training and 2–4 weeks of hypercare. After that, your team runs it, or we look after it on a monthly support retainer.

Do you do marketing or social media?

No. We build data and AI systems. If that's what you need, we'll happily refer you to a specialist.
