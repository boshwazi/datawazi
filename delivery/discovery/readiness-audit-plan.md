## What this audit answers
1. Where does your data live today, and how much can you trust it?
2. Which problems are worth solving first, and what will they change?
3. What should you build, fix or leave alone, and in what order?

## What you get
- **Readiness report**: findings across systems, data quality, people, process, security and PDPA.
- **Prioritised roadmap**: 3–6 initiatives, each with its business outcome, effort, dependencies and a suggested order.
- **Readout session**: 60–90 minutes with your sponsor and key people.

## How we treat your data
We work read-only, take the minimum sample needed, mask personal data where possible, and keep everything in {{AGREED_STORAGE}}. Samples are deleted at the end of the audit unless you ask us to keep them. See the PDPA checklist.

## Interview guide (45 min)
1. Your role, and the decisions you make each week or month
2. The reports and data you rely on. Where do they come from?
3. What takes too long, breaks, or can't be trusted?
4. If you had a reliable answer to one question, what would it be?
5. Where could automation or AI help, and where should it stay out?
6. Anything sensitive we should know about: personal data, regulators, audits?
