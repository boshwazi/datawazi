## 1. Purpose
AI tools can save time and improve our work. This policy explains how everyone at {{CLIENT_NAME}} can use them **well and safely**: protecting personal data, confidential information, our customers and our reputation.

## 2. Who and what it covers
- **Everyone** who works for or with {{CLIENT_NAME}}: staff, contractors, interns and vendors working on our behalf.
- **All generative AI tools** that create text, images, code, audio or analysis, including chatbots, assistants built into other software (e.g. email, office or meeting tools) and browser extensions.

## 4. What information can go into AI tools: the traffic light
| Colour | Information | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **GREEN · go** | Public or harmless: published information, generic text, your own writing with no names or figures | Any approved tool |
| **AMBER · check** | Internal, not sensitive: internal procedures, anonymised or summary data, internal drafts | Approved tools with a work account only |
| **RED · stop** | Personal data (names with IC/passport numbers, contact details, salaries, health, biometric, financial or customer records), confidential business information (contracts, unreleased financials, tender documents, legal advice), security information (passwords, keys, system details) | **Not allowed**, unless a specific system has been approved in writing for that data |

**When unsure, treat it as RED and ask.** Removing names and identifiers can turn RED information into AMBER.

## 5. Using AI output
1. **A person checks everything** before it is used, sent or published: facts, numbers, names, dates, tone and data.
2. **You are accountable** for anything you send or decide, whether or not AI helped.
3. **Be open about it.** Say when AI generated most of a document, image or message that goes outside {{CLIENT_NAME}}. {{Adjust: e.g. internal disclosure not required.}}
4. **Respect others' rights.** Don't use AI to copy or imitate someone else's work, brand, voice or likeness.
5. **Keep records** of AI-assisted work as you would any other work. Prompts containing AMBER information are stored only in approved tools.

## 6. Not allowed
- Entering RED information into any tool not approved for it
- Using AI to make or fully automate **decisions about people**: hiring, performance, discipline, credit, eligibility or access to services
- Presenting AI output as verified fact without checking it
- Creating misleading, discriminatory, harassing or unlawful content
- Getting around security controls, or using AI tools to access systems or data you are not authorised to see
- Connecting AI tools (plug-ins, extensions, integrations) to company systems or data without IT approval

## 7. Building AI into systems or services
AI features in our products, services or internal systems (e.g. chatbots for customers, assistants on internal documents) need a short assessment **before launch**: purpose, data used, where data is processed and stored, accuracy testing, human oversight, how users are told, and who monitors it. Approval: {{APPROVER}}.

## 8. Personal data and PDPA
- Personal data is used only for the purpose it was collected for, and kept secure, including when an AI tool processes it.
- Transfers of personal data outside Malaysia follow {{CLIENT_NAME}}'s data protection policy.
- Suspected exposure of personal data through an AI tool is a **data incident**: report it immediately (section 9).
