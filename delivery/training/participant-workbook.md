- **The traffic light**, before you paste anything:

| Colour | What it is | Examples | Where it can go |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GREEN · go** | Public or harmless | Published information, generic text, your own writing with no names or numbers | Any approved AI tool |
| **AMBER · check** | Internal, not sensitive | Internal procedures, anonymised or summary data, internal drafts | Company-approved tools only, with a work account |
| **RED · stop** | Personal or confidential | Names with IC numbers, salaries, health or customer data, contracts, unreleased financials, passwords | Never, unless a specific system is approved for it |

**Unsure? Treat it as red and ask.** Removing names and identifiers can turn red into amber.

## The 5-part brief
Brief the AI like a capable new colleague on their first day.

| | Part | Ask yourself | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Goal** | What do I want, and who is it for? | "Write a short reply to a customer…" |
| 2 | **Context** | What does it need to know about me and the situation? | "…a long-standing customer whose order is 5 days late…" |
| 3 | **Material** | What should it work from? (traffic light!) | "Key facts: new date 28 Nov, free delivery on this order." |
| 4 | **Format** | Length, tone, structure, language? | "Under 120 words, warm but professional." |
| 5 | **Check** | Should it ask questions or flag gaps first? | "Ask me if anything is unclear before writing." |

**Follow-up moves:** "Shorter." · "More formal." · "As a table with columns…" · "In Bahasa Melayu, formal register." · "What's missing?" · "What would a sceptical reader ask?"

## The 6-point check (before anything goes out)
☐ **Facts:** true, and from a source I trust?
☐ **Numbers:** recalculated myself, not copied?
☐ **Names, dates, titles:** correct?
☐ **Tone:** right for this reader?
☐ **Data:** nothing red in it?
☐ **Would I sign it** without the AI?
