# Fit-check call guide (30 min) 🔒

> 🛈 Internal. This is how we run the free 30-minute fit check. Goal: get answers to the 20 intake questions, decide if we're the right fit, and agree one clear next step. Take notes straight into `Client_Intake_Quick.xlsx`.

## Before the call (10 min)
- [ ] Look up the organisation: sector, size, any public annual report or news.
- [ ] If they filled in the client intake form, read it and mark the gaps.
- [ ] Have the pitch deck ready (`sales/deck/`). Only show it if they ask "who are you / what do you do".

## Running order

| Time | Part | What to cover | Intake Qs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Open | Thanks, agenda, "by the end you'll have a clear next step, even if it isn't us." | – |
| 3–12 | The problem | What isn't working? Why now? What does success look like *in numbers*? | 1–3 |
| 12–16 | People | Who decides and owns the budget? Who uses it? Who else is involved? | 4–6 |
| 16–22 | Data & tech | Where does the data live? How messy is it? Any personal data? What tools today? | 9–17 |
| 22–26 | What, when, budget | What first? Budget range? Running cost? Deadlines? | 7–8, 18–20 |
| 26–30 | Close | Play back the problem in one sentence. Say what happens next and when. | – |

## Good follow-up questions
- "If we fixed only one thing, what would it be?" (Q8)
- "How do you produce that report today, step by step?"
- "Who would notice first if the number was wrong?"
- "Has anyone tried to fix this before? What happened?"
- "Is the budget approved, or does this need a business case first?"

## Budget question (Q18): how to ask it
> "To recommend the right size of solution, it helps to know roughly what range you have in mind. Under RM50k, RM50–150k, or above?"

Never quote prices on the call. If asked: *"It depends on scope. I'll send a clear recommendation within two working days."*

## Close options (pick one)
1. **Proposal**: the request is small and clear. Say: "I'll send a short proposal by {{DATE}}."
2. **Readiness audit**: the problem is real but the path isn't clear yet. Offer the 1–2 week paid audit.
3. **Not now**: there's no budget or sponsor yet. Offer to stay in touch and send a useful resource.
4. **Refer out**: not our work (for example marketing or social media, or pure hardware/IT support). Name who could help.

## After the call (same day)
- [ ] Complete `Client_Intake_Quick.xlsx`, including the "Our first read" section.
- [ ] Score it with `fit-check/first-read-scorecard.md`.
- [ ] Send email 3 (go) or email 4 (refer out) within 2 working days.
